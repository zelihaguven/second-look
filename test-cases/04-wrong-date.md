# 04 — Wrong date

## Answer

The Berlin Wall fell on 9 November 1991, two years after the Soviet Union dissolved. Its fall led directly to German reunification, which was completed the following year.

## Expected

- `fact` or `number`, **high**: "fell on 9 November 1991". The Wall fell on 9 November 1989.
- "two years after the Soviet Union dissolved" is also wrong (the USSR dissolved in December 1991, after the Wall fell).
