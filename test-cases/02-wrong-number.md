# 02 — Wrong number

## Answer

The Eiffel Tower is 512 metres tall, which made it the tallest man-made structure in the world until the Chrysler Building was finished in 1930. It was built for the 1889 World's Fair and took just over two years to complete.

## Expected

- `number`, **high**: "The Eiffel Tower is 512 metres tall". It is about 330 m including antennas (300 m originally).
- The 1889 World's Fair and the Chrysler Building in 1930 are correct, so `low` or `medium` is fine for those.
