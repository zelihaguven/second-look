# 03 — Fake paper

## Answer

Studying with background music hurts memory. A 2018 study by Henderson and Liu in Nature Neuroscience ("Auditory load and episodic encoding", vol. 21, pp. 1123–1131) found that students who studied with lyrical music recalled 37% fewer facts than students who studied in silence.

## Expected

- `citation`, **high**: the Henderson and Liu 2018 *Nature Neuroscience* paper. It is fabricated.
- `number`, **high** or **medium**: "37% fewer facts".
